# İslamic Source - Daily & Weekly Planner

*As-salamu alaykum wa rahmatullahi wa barakatuh*  
Bismillah.

Dear brothers and sisters, I am very pleased to present to you our Daily and Weekly Planner. With this work, prepared in the light of the sleep, sports, and program guides that we have offered you on our website in recent weeks, you can easily prepare a daily and weekly program for yourself. Without further ado, let us explain what we offer you with this planner:

- Daily program, daily sports program, and weekly program in one place.
- Two different designs in 3 languages, as pre-filled or blank.
- Prepared in A5 size, compact design. You can also print it in A4 size if you wish.
- Daily program section colored in daylight style.
- Weekly program designed for you to determine your weekly tasks.
- Sports program spread throughout the day and easy to implement.
- Notes section designed for you to write/stick any notes you wish.
- With its foldable form, you can carry it anywhere you want or divide it and use separately.
- Descriptive and simple design.
- Flexible program divided according to prayer times.
- Weekly program prepared based on rest days as well.
- Program structure that can be applied in all circumstances, allows a margin of error, and provides flexibility.
- All programs are designed to be universal, applicable even for those working 08:00-18:00 or going to school.
- Prepared with scholarly and scientific data, lived experiences, and real-life knowledge from the guide trilogy.
- Completely free and customizable.

Our planner has been prepared with the free and open-source GIMP program. If you wish, you can download the project files of the planner and customize and edit anything you want. You can also access images related to example usage scenarios of our planner from here.

If we have been correct, it is only from Allah. If we have erred, it is only from us. Insha'Allah, it has been beneficial.  
With the hope that it will be a means for good. We await your prayers.  
*As-salamu alaykum.*
